# AI Operations Platform

## Master Project Plan

Version 2.0

Author: Maria Fe Blanca

---

# Executive Summary

The AI Operations Platform (AIOP) is an enterprise-grade automation framework that combines Artificial Intelligence, Google Workspace, Google Apps Script, and external business systems into a unified operational platform.

Rather than functioning as a traditional chatbot, AIOP acts as an intelligent operations layer that understands company knowledge, automates repetitive work, assists employees, and orchestrates workflows across multiple business applications.

The platform is designed to be industry-independent. By changing the knowledge base and business integrations, the same architecture can support property management, construction, oil & gas, logistics, healthcare, customer success, and professional services.

---

# Vision

To create an AI-powered operational ecosystem where every employee has access to an intelligent digital coworker capable of understanding company processes, retrieving institutional knowledge, generating documentation, coordinating workflows, and assisting with daily operations.

The platform augments human expertise rather than replacing it, ensuring that routine work is automated while business-critical decisions remain under human control.

---

# Mission

Build a modular, scalable, and secure AI platform that transforms static documentation and disconnected software into an intelligent operational environment.

The platform should:

• Reduce repetitive administrative work.

• Improve operational consistency.

• Accelerate onboarding.

• Preserve organizational knowledge.

• Standardize documentation.

• Enhance decision support.

• Integrate seamlessly with existing business applications.

---

# Platform Objectives

The platform has six primary objectives.

## Knowledge

Transform SOPs, policies, manuals, contracts, and documentation into a searchable AI knowledge base.

---

## Automation

Automate repetitive workflows using Google Apps Script, APIs, scheduled jobs, and event-driven triggers.

---

## Intelligence

Use Claude as the reasoning engine for classification, summarization, drafting, recommendation, and decision support.

---

## Integration

Connect business systems into a single operational ecosystem.

Examples include:

• Gmail

• Spark

• Google Workspace

• Buildium

• AppFolio

• Dialpad

• Slack

• ClickUp

• Airtable

• QuickBooks

• Future ERP and CRM systems

---

## Collaboration

Improve communication between departments through shared AI assistants, standardized workflows, and centralized operational knowledge.

---

## Scalability

Support organizations ranging from a single operator to enterprise teams across multiple departments and industries.

---

# Core Principles

The platform is governed by six principles.

1. AI assists people; it does not replace accountability.

2. Every workflow is modular.

3. Every integration is API-first.

4. Every automation is observable and logged.

5. Company knowledge remains the primary source of truth.

6. Human approval is required for critical business decisions.

---

# Platform Layers

The platform is organized into six architectural layers.

Business Layer

Knowledge Layer

AI Layer

Automation Layer

Integration Layer

Infrastructure Layer

Each layer is independent, allowing the platform to evolve without requiring major redesign.

# Target Outcomes

The long-term goals of the platform include:

• Significant reduction in repetitive administrative work.

• Faster employee onboarding through AI-assisted knowledge retrieval.

• Standardized documentation and communications.

• Improved operational visibility through centralized reporting.

• Reduced response times for internal and customer-facing workflows.

• A reusable platform that can be adapted to multiple industries without changing the underlying architecture.

---

# Initial Industry Implementation

The first production implementation will focus on HOA and Property Management operations.

This implementation will leverage the company's existing SOPs and workflows to validate the platform architecture, automation framework, and AI capabilities before expanding to additional industries.

---

# Long-Term Roadmap

The AI Operations Platform is intended to evolve into a comprehensive enterprise operating system capable of supporting operational, administrative, and knowledge-intensive work across diverse organizations.

Future enhancements include advanced analytics, voice assistants, predictive automation, multi-tenant deployments, industry-specific modules, and additional AI capabilities.

---

# Conclusion

The AI Operations Platform is more than an automation project. It is a strategic framework for building intelligent operational systems that combine organizational knowledge, AI reasoning, workflow automation, and business applications into a single, extensible platform capable of supporting organizations for years to come.
